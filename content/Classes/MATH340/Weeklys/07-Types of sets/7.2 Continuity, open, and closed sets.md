## Theorem 7.4
Let $f : \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^m$ be a function

1. $f$ is [[6.2 Continuity#6.6 Continuity|continuous]] iff for any given open subset of $U$ of $\mathbb{R}^m$, the inverse image $f^{-1}(U)$ is an [[7.1 Topology of Rn#7.1 Open sets|open]] subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$

2. $f$ is [[6.2 Continuity#6.6 Continuity|continuous]] iff if given any closed subset $C$ of $\mathbb{R}^m$ the inverse image $f^{-1}(C)$ is a [[7.1 Topology of Rn#7.2 Closed sets|closed]] subset $\mathbb{R}^n$

'if a subset in of codomain is open, then its pre-image will also be open'
'if a subset of the codomain is closed, then the pre-image of that subset will be closed'
