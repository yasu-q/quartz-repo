## Theorem 14.3
Let $P$ be a point in $\mathbb{R}^3$, and ${\bf F}$ be a vector field that is continuously differentiable on a open ball centered at $P$. Let $S_a$ denote the sphere of radius a centered at $P$ , oriented outward. Then
$$
\text{div } {\bf F}(P) = \lim\limits_{a \rightarrow 0^+} \frac{3}{4\pi a^3} \iint_{S_a} {\bf F} \cdot d{\bf S}
$$
I.e. divergence ([[14.1 Stokes's and Gauss' Theorems#14.2 Divergence]]) is sort of the "rate at which a vector field 'points out' at a point $P$" It's a differential form itself.
## Theorem 14.4
Let $P$ be a point in $\mathbb{R}^3$, and ${\bf F}$ be a vector field that is continuously differentiable on a open ball centered at $P$. Suppose ${\bf n}$ is a unit vector from $P$ . Let $C_a$ denote the circle of radius a centered at $P$ that lines in the plane perpendicular to the vector ${\bf n}$. Then, the component of curl ${\bf F}$ ([[13.2 Conservative vector fields#13.5 Curl]]) in the direction of ${\bf n}$ is
$$
{\bf n} \cdot \text{curl } {\bf F}(P) = \lim\limits_{a \rightarrow 0^+} = \frac{1}{\pi a^2} \int_{C_a} {\bf F} \cdot d{\bf s}
$$
Where $C_a$ is oriented with the right hand rule with respect to ${\bf n}$

i.e. curl is tendency of a vector field to "rotate" at a point $P$
